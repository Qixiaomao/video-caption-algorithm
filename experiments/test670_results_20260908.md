# Frozen three-seed MSVD test670 evaluation: results

Completed 2026-09-08 under the committed
`experiments/test670_protocol_20260908.md` protocol.

## Decision

**The repaired caption+InfoNCE method transfers to the held-out MSVD test
split.** Across three paired seeds, InfoNCE improved every BLEU score, ROUGE-L,
CIDEr, and every registered retrieval recall. Caption METEOR was mixed and
decreased slightly on average.

The result supports a paper claim of improved visual-text alignment with
preserved, modestly improved aggregate caption quality. It does not support a
claim that every caption metric improves, that human semantic quality improves,
or that the model is state of the art.

## Frozen evaluation

- Test split: 670 unique videos and 27,763 references.
- Checkpoints: epoch 66 from caption-only A and caption+InfoNCE B for seeds
  123, 124, and 125.
- Decoder: empty prompt, greedy, one beam, no sampling, maximum 24 tokens,
  no-repeat 3-gram, repetition penalty 1.0.
- Caption metrics: `pycocoevalcap==1.2`, PTB tokenization, all references per
  video. Scores below use the conventional raw-score-times-100 scale.
- Retrieval: all 27,763 captions query 670 candidate videos; video-to-text uses
  all matching captions as positives.
- No test-based checkpoint, seed, epoch, decoding, lambda, or temperature
  selection was performed.

## Prediction-level boundary

InfoNCE changed the normalized greedy caption for 257/670 videos in seed 123,
448/670 in seed 124, and 380/670 in seed 125. Unique-caption counts changed
from 425 to 412, 409 to 404, and 400 to 395, respectively. Mean caption length
also decreased slightly in each seed. These facts reinforce that the method
changes caption behavior, but automatic corpus scores alone do not establish
better human semantic grounding. A bounded qualitative audit remains useful
before writing examples into the paper.

## Paper interpretation

The strongest supported method claim is not that InfoNCE makes GPT-2 a much
better caption generator. The supported claim is narrower and more useful:
under complete caption supervision on a consumer 4GB GPU, the fixed auxiliary
InfoNCE objective consistently improves held-out visual-text retrieval while
preserving caption quality and producing small gains in BLEU, ROUGE-L, and
CIDEr. METEOR remains flat to slightly negative.

This makes the project viable as a lightweight multimodal alignment and
systems paper when combined with the existing latency, throughput, memory,
and profiling evidence. It is weaker as a pure caption-quality paper because
the caption gains are small and the architecture is not competitive with
modern large video-language models on novelty alone.

## Boundaries and next action

- Three seeds and one frozen InfoNCE setting; no hyperparameter sweep.
- One standard MSVD split and one fixed greedy decoder.
- COCO-caption metrics are sensitive to tokenization and preprocessing; only
  compare literature values that use a compatible split and evaluator.
- The retrieval representation is method-defined and must be described fully.
- No statistical significance claim is made from three seeds.
- No human evaluation has been performed.
- The next justified work is a bounded qualitative caption audit and a paper
  results-table rewrite. Do not reopen test670 for tuning.
